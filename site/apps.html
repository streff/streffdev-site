DoseTempo

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

DoseTempo reminds you when your next dose is due. It was built for people juggling several medicines on different schedules, including ones taken every few hours rather than at set times of day.

  • Reminders at any interval, from every few hours to every few days.
  • Doses due at the same time are grouped into one reminder.
  • Missed doses keep reminding you until you respond.
  • Every dose is logged as taken, skipped or missed.
  • No account, no adverts and no tracking. Your medicines and history stay on your phone, with password-protected backups for moving to a new one.

Free for up to four medicines. A single one-off purchase of £2.99 unlocks unlimited medicines, PDF dose reports for your GP or pharmacist, stock tracking with repeat-prescription warnings, and tapering plans. There is no subscription.

DoseTempo does not give medical advice, calculate doses or check interactions. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
