About streffdev

streffdev is the trading name of Streffdev Ltd, a software company registered in Scotland. It was set up to build and publish focused apps for people and businesses.

How we build

We understand the real problem first, keep the solution as simple as it can be, and test it properly before anyone relies on it.

What we believe

  • Software should do its job and then get out of the way.
  • Your data belongs to you. We collect as little as possible.
  • Prices should be clear and fair, especially for apps people rely on for their health.
