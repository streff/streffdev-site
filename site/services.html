Services

We build software for two kinds of customer: people who want an app that does one thing properly, and organisations that need a tool shaped around the way they work.

Apps for individuals

Our own apps start from a real problem, usually one that the existing options make harder than it needs to be. They are designed to be simple to use, respectful of your privacy, and fairly priced.

  • Data stays on your device unless you choose to export it.
  • No adverts and no selling of personal information.
  • One-off purchases rather than subscriptions wherever the app allows it.

Software for businesses

Mobile apps

Native-quality apps for iPhone and Android, built from a single codebase where that makes sense and published under your own name or ours.

Web apps

Browser-based tools for customers or staff, from booking and enquiry forms to dashboards and self-service portals.

Bespoke business tools

Replacements for the spreadsheets, shared inboxes and paper forms that an expanding team outgrows, plus integrations that move data between the systems you already pay for.

Process and requirements

Before anything is built, we map how the work happens today, agree what needs to change, and write it down as clear requirements you can check. It is the cheapest point in a project to get things right.

How a project runs

  1. Understand the problemA conversation about what you need, who will use it and what success looks like.
  2. Agree what to buildA short written specification with scope, cost and timescale, so there are no surprises.
  3. Build in small releasesYou see working software early and often, and can change direction before it costs much.
  4. Hand over and supportDocumentation, app store publishing if needed, and an agreed level of ongoing support.
